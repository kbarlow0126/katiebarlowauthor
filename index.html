Meet Katie

Stories inspired by home, family, and small-town wonder.

Katie Barlow is a children's author who writes warm, imaginative stories filled with friendship, curiosity, and adventure.

Her work is inspired by Virginia's Shenandoah Valley, the town of Stanley, and the unforgettable moments that make childhood special. When she is not writing, Katie enjoys time with her two boys, serving her community, and dreaming up the next story.
